# NutriSphere — Dietitian User Manual

## Welcome to the Dietitian Portal
The Dietitian Portal enables clinical nutritionists to design evidence-based dietary interventions, monitor patient compliance, and leverage clinical intelligence to adapt plans safely.

---

## 1. Dashboard Overview (`/dietitian/dashboard`)
Upon logging in as a Dietitian:
- **Caseload Summary**: Displays total active patients, patients requiring attention, and plans pending review.
- **Adherence Breakdown**: High-level adherence percentages across your patient roster.
- **Quick Actions**: "Create New Plan", "Calculate Requirements", "Review Adaptive Tickets".

## 2. Managing Patients (`/dietitian/patients`)
- View patient lists with search and status filters (`Active`, `Attention Needed`).
- Click on any patient to view their full clinical profile, medical conditions diagnosed by Doctors, and current Reality Score.

## 3. Creating & Approving Diet Plans (`/dietitian/diet-plans`)
1. Click **Create Diet Plan**.
2. Select target patient and enter daily caloric, protein, carbohydrate, and fat targets.
3. Add scheduled meals (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Snacks) with portion sizes and target calories.
4. Click **Submit & Approve** to activate the prescription for the patient.

## 4. Reviewing Adaptive Recommendations (`/dietitian/adaptive`)
- The Adaptive Engine flags issues (e.g. `Low Hydration Pattern`, `Protein Deficit`, `Recurring Barrier`).
- Inspect the rationale, detected pattern, and suggested change.
- Click **Approve** to accept the suggestion or **Reject** to dismiss with custom clinical review notes.
