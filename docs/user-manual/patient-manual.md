# NutriSphere — Patient User Manual

## Welcome to Your NutriSphere Companion
NutriSphere is your daily partner for clinical nutrition, helping you stay on track with meals prescribed by your Dietitian and Doctor.

---

## 1. Dashboard (`/patient/dashboard`)
- **Daily Progress Bar**: Shows today's consumed calories vs. daily target (e.g. 1500 / 2000 kcal).
- **Macro Rings**: Protein, Carbs, and Fats meters.
- **Hydration Tracker**: Visual water gauge showing ml consumed toward your 2000 ml goal.
- **Reality Score Card**: Shows your current plan feasibility rating.

## 2. Daily Logging
- **Food Logging (`/patient/food-logging`)**: Search for foods or enter custom meals with portion sizes.
- **Water Logging (`/patient/water-logging`)**: One-tap buttons (+250ml, +500ml) to log drinking water.
- **Meal Deviations & Barriers (`/patient/barriers`)**: If life gets in the way, log what happened (e.g., "Work meeting ran late"). Your Dietitian sees this and adjusts future plans.

## 3. Home Food Mode (`/patient/home-food`)
- When you can't go grocery shopping, tap **Home Food Mode**.
- Add the ingredients you currently have in your fridge or pantry (e.g. eggs, rice, spinach).
- The system immediately suggests compliant meals you can cook right away without breaking your diet plan!

## 4. Hotel Kitchen Room Service (`/patient/hotel/menu`)
- When staying at an affiliated hotel or hospital room, browse clinically approved meals crafted by the kitchen.
- Select your meal, specify delivery instructions, and place an order.
- Track preparation and delivery in real-time under **My Orders**.

## 5. Nutrition Digital Twin (`/patient/digital-twin`)
- View a simulation of your 30-day projected weight and metabolic trajectory based on your daily logging habits!
