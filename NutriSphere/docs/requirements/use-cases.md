# NutriSphere — Master Clinical Use Cases

| UC-ID | Use Case Title | Primary Actor | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| **UC-01** | Clinical Consultation & Condition Recording | Doctor | Doctor examines patient, inputs diagnosis, and sets medical constraints. |
| **UC-02** | Diagnostic Laboratory Entry | Doctor | Doctor inputs blood test parameters (HbA1c, lipid panel, creatinine). |
| **UC-03** | Nutritional Assessment & Requirement Calc | Dietitian | Dietitian runs Harris-Benedict/Mifflin calculator to establish caloric targets. |
| **UC-04** | Diet Plan Authoring & Approval | Dietitian | Dietitian designs meal schedule with calories and macronutrients, then approves it. |
| **UC-05** | Daily Food & Hydration Logging | Patient | Patient records breakfast, lunch, dinner, snacks, and water intake throughout the day. |
| **UC-06** | Adherence Barrier Reporting | Patient | Patient logs an obstacle (e.g. time constraint) preventing compliance. |
| **UC-07** | Reality Score Evaluation | Dietitian / Patient | System computes feasibility score across 6 behavioral dimensions. |
| **UC-08** | Adaptive Recommendation Review | Dietitian | Dietitian reviews automated adjustment tickets and approves or rejects changes. |
| **UC-09** | Home Food Pantry Recipe Match | Patient | Patient registers pantry ingredients; system synthesizes compliant meals. |
| **UC-10** | Culinary Kitchen Meal Order | Patient | Patient browses hotel kitchen menu and orders compliant room delivery. |
| **UC-11** | Kitchen Order Fulfillment | Hotel Staff | Kitchen updates preparation status from `PENDING` to `COMPLETED`. |
| **UC-12** | Digital Twin Simulation Inspection | Patient / Clinician | User reviews 30-day projected weight trajectory and metabolic stability flags. |
