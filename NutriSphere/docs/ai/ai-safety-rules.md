# NutriSphere — AI Clinical Safety Rules & Guardrails

## Core Safety Tenet
**No AI suggestion or automated algorithm may directly modify, replace, or cancel an approved clinical diet plan or prescription.** All intelligent adaptations must follow the Human-In-The-Loop (HITL) protocol.

---

## Safety Guardrails Checklist

### 1. Human-In-The-Loop (HITL) Enforcement
- Automated recommendations generated by the `AdaptiveEngineService` default to `AdaptiveRecommendationStatus.PENDING_REVIEW`.
- Only users with role `ROLE_DIETITIAN` can transition status to `APPROVED` or `REJECTED`.
- Patients receive notifications of potential changes only after Dietitian sign-off.

### 2. Caloric & Macronutrient Boundary Limits
- Automated adjustments must not suggest caloric intake below:
  - Female floor: 1,200 kcal/day (unless under direct medical supervision).
  - Male floor: 1,500 kcal/day.
- Protein recommendations must not exceed 2.5g per kg of body weight for non-renal patients.
- Renal impairment flag (`CKD_STAGE_3+` or `eGFR < 60`) locks protein modifications and alerts the assigned Nephrologist/Dietitian.

### 3. Allergen Exclusion Zero-Tolerance
- Hard constraint: If a food item contains an allergen recorded in `patient_profiles.allergies` or `patient_conditions`, it is excluded with a zero-tolerance filter before reaching recipe suggestions.

### 4. Deterministic Clinical Math
- Clinical values (BMR, TDEE, macronutrient grams) are strictly calculated using validated medical formulas:
  - **Mifflin-St Jeor**:
    - Men: $BMR = (10 \times \text{weight in kg}) + (6.25 \times \text{height in cm}) - (5 \times \text{age}) + 5$
    - Women: $BMR = (10 \times \text{weight in kg}) + (6.25 \times \text{height in cm}) - (5 \times \text{age}) - 161$
- LLMs are never used to perform arithmetic on caloric or nutritional totals.
